# HelloGitHub 月刊

> Last checked: 2026-10 against the [HelloGitHub README](https://github.com/521xueweihan/HelloGitHub)
> and [hellogithub.com](https://hellogithub.com/periodical).

## Fit

HelloGitHub shares **interesting, beginner-friendly open-source projects** (有趣、入门级的
开源项目) plus open-source books, hands-on projects and enterprise projects. It publishes
**on the 28th of every month** as a monthly issue, on GitHub, its website and its WeChat
official account. Being featured brings a steady stream of Chinese readers and a
"Featured｜HelloGitHub" badge you can put in your README.

## How to submit

The README says recommendations and **self-recommendations (推荐或自荐)** are welcome,
through the **「提交项目」** entry on [hellogithub.com](https://hellogithub.com/periodical).
Selection is editorial; there's no guarantee and no published checklist, so make the
project easy to evaluate.

## What helps

- A **Chinese README** (or a Chinese section) — the audience reads Chinese first.
- A clear "what is it / who is it for / how to start in one step" at the top.
- Screenshots or a GIF.
- Fun or learning value: the monthly favours projects that are interesting to try or
  good to learn from, not just enterprise infrastructure.
- Active maintenance: recent commits and a release.

## Template (submission description)

```
项目名称：<名字>
仓库地址：<链接>
一句话介绍：<给谁用，解决什么问题>

推荐理由（自荐）：
- 有趣 / 实用的点：<具体说明>
- 上手方式：<一行命令或在线体验链接>
- 适合人群：<例如：想给自己的开源项目做推广、但不熟悉各个社区规则的开发者>
```

## Pitfalls

- An English-only README with no Chinese explanation → harder for editors and readers.
- Submitting before there's anything to try → "interesting" is judged by trying it.
- Submitting the same project every month → one good submission is enough; resubmit
  only after significant updates.
