# X (Twitter) and Bluesky

> Last checked: 2026-10. No special rules beyond each platform's spam policy.

## Fit

Developers who already follow you, people in your niche, and the accounts that share
dev tools. Reach depends on your network, so a good post *plus* a reply from a relevant
account beats a long thread from a cold account.

## Format

- **Lead with a video or GIF** of the aha moment (10–30 seconds, no intro). Native
  video outperforms a link card.
- **First line** = the outcome, in plain words. Put the link in the post or in the
  first reply (people disagree about which reaches more; test both).
- **Threads**: 3–6 posts. 1) demo + outcome, 2) why you built it, 3) how it works,
  4) how to try it + link, 5) what's next / ask for feedback.
- Tag tools or libraries you genuinely built on — their maintainers often reshare.
- Use 0–2 hashtags at most; they rarely help on X.

## Build in public

Posting progress (screenshots, numbers, mistakes) in the weeks before launch builds
the audience that shows up on launch day. The launch post can then quote-post your
first progress post.

## Template

```
1/ <Name> turns <input> into <output> in <time>. Open source. 👇
[demo video]

2/ I built it after <specific moment>. <Alternative> needed <pain>.

3/ How it works: <interesting technical bit>.

4/ Try it: <command or link>
   Repo: <link>

5/ It doesn't do <X> yet. What should I build next?
```

## Pitfalls

- A thread with no visual → scrolled past.
- Tagging big accounts asking for retweets → looks like spam.
- Posting once and disappearing → reply to every quote and reply for the first hours.
