# Newsletters

> Last checked: 2026-10. Submission routes below were confirmed on each site; editors
> pick what they like, so a submission is a suggestion, not a guarantee.

## Fit

Curated newsletters put your project in front of tens of thousands of subscribers who
opted in to hear about new tools — without you needing an audience. Submitting costs
minutes. Do it in the week of your launch so the issue lands while you're active.

## Where to submit

| Newsletter | Audience | How to submit |
|---|---|---|
| [This Week in Rust](https://github.com/rust-lang/this-week-in-rust) | Rust | PR updating the relevant section of the draft in `drafts/`. **Links that are only a repo or crate aren't accepted** — link a write-up with Rust-specific content (examples, what you learned) |
| [PyCoder's Weekly](https://pycoders.com/submissions) | Python | "Submit Your Link" form; your own projects are welcome |
| [Golang Weekly](https://golangweekly.com), [JavaScript Weekly](https://javascriptweekly.com) and their sister weeklies | Go, JS, Node, frontend… | Cooperpress weeklies — check the site footer/about page for the current submission route |
| [Changelog News](https://changelog.com/news/submit) | Open source and software broadly | Submit form |
| [Console](https://console.dev/selection-criteria) | Developer tools | Email hello@console.dev; picks 2–3 tools a week that individuals can sign up for without talking to sales, plus a separate list for pre-1.0 betas |
| [阮一峰《科技爱好者周刊》](ruanyf-weekly.md) | Chinese tech readers | GitHub issue — see its playbook |
| [HelloGitHub 月刊](hellogithub.md) | Chinese open-source readers | Website submission — see its playbook |

Also look for the newsletter of your exact niche (e.g. a framework, a database, an editor);
small, focused newsletters often convert better than big general ones.

## Template (email or form)

```
Subject: <Name> – <one-line what it does>

Hi <editor>,

I'm the author of <Name>, an open-source <category> that <outcome> for <audience>.

What's interesting about it: <the one thing a reader of THIS newsletter would care about>.

Repo: <link> · Demo: <link> · License: <license>

Thanks for considering it!
<name>
```

## Pitfalls

- Submitting before the README and demo are ready — editors click once.
- Mass-mailing the same text to every newsletter. Lead with what fits each one.
- Following up repeatedly. One polite submission is enough.
