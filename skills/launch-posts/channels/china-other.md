# Other Chinese channels: 知乎, B 站, 开源中国, 少数派, Gitee

> Last checked: 2026-10. These are long-tail channels — slower than V2EX or 即刻, but
> content there keeps getting found through search. Check each platform's current
> rules before posting.

## 知乎 (Zhihu)

- **Answer existing questions** where your project genuinely solves the asker's problem
  ("有哪些好用的开源 XXX 工具？", "如何推广自己的开源项目？"). Give a real answer first,
  then mention your project and **say you're the author**.
- A column article (专栏) works like a 掘金 article: problem → approach → result → link.
- Don't paste the same answer under many questions.

## B 站 (Bilibili)

- **Video demos and tutorials**: a 3–8 minute "从零用 <名字> 做 X" video, or a 1-minute
  demo. Developer and AI-tool content has an active audience.
- Put the repo link in the video description (简介) and pin a comment with it.
- Chapters (分段) and a clear cover title help search.

## 开源中国 (OSChina)

- Runs a large **open-source software directory** ([oschina.net/project](https://www.oschina.net/project))
  and news section that many Chinese developers browse. Look for the submission entry for
  adding a project (投递/收录) and follow its format.
- Useful for long-tail discovery by Chinese-speaking users and for projects aimed at
  domestic enterprises.

## 少数派 (sspai)

- For **end-user apps and productivity tools** (not libraries). Its community writing
  area accepts user-written articles; an honest "how I use it" piece with screenshots
  fits better than an announcement.

## Gitee

- A Chinese code-hosting platform. A **mirror of your repo** on Gitee helps users with
  slow access to GitHub, and Gitee has its own recommended-projects listings. Keep the
  GitHub repo as the source of truth and say so in both READMEs.

## Pitfalls

- Same copy everywhere → each platform has its own format; adapt.
- Not disclosing authorship in answers → reported as 软广.
