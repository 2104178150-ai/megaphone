# Lobsters

> Last checked: 2026-10 against the [about page](https://lobste.rs/about). Lobsters is
> invite-only; you need an existing member to invite you.

## Fit

A small, highly technical community focused on computing. Deep technical write-ups,
programming-language and systems work do well. Marketing and product announcements
do badly.

## Rules

- Every story needs **accurate tags**; off-topic or mis-tagged stories get flagged.
- **Self-promotion is limited.** Authors are welcome to take part, but not to use the
  site as a write-only promotion tool. The about page's rule of thumb: self-promo should
  be **less than a quarter** of your stories and comments. Tick the "I am the author"
  box when submitting your own work.
- The quickest way to get an invitation is to talk to someone you recognise from the
  site. New users can't send invitations during their first 70 days, and inviters are
  accountable for the people they invite — don't put a friend in a spot by spamming.

## Format

- Link to a technical article or the repo, with a plain descriptive title.
- If you're the author, a short comment explaining the motivation is welcome.

## Pitfalls

- Posting a product launch rather than something technically interesting.
- Asking for an invite just to promote your project.
