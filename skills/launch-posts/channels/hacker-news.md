# Hacker News — Show HN

> Last checked: 2026-10 against [Show HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html),
> [HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) and the [FAQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsfaq.html).

## Fit

Technical, curious, skeptical readers who love things they can try right now. Wins:
clever tools, clean implementations, small projects with a sharp idea, honest write-ups
of how something was built. Loses: marketing copy, sign-up walls, "AI-powered" with no
substance, landing pages.

## Rules (from HN itself)

- Show HN is for **something people can try**: software they can run, or hardware they
  can hold (a video or detailed write-up is fine for hardware).
- It must be **non-trivial**, **built by you**, and you must be **around to discuss it**.
- Users should be able to try it **without signing up or giving an email**.
- **Not** for blog posts, sign-up pages, newsletters, lists or other reading material —
  submit those as normal stories. Landing pages and fundraisers don't qualify.
- Routine feature releases and version bumps don't qualify; a major overhaul may.
- Title starts with **"Show HN:"**. Explain **how and why** you built it.
- **Don't ask friends to upvote or comment.** Vote-rings are detected and penalised.
- HN isn't primarily for promotion: post your own work only part of the time.
- Reposts: if a story hasn't had significant attention in the last year or so, a small
  number of reposts is OK; otherwise they're buried as duplicates.

## Format

- **Title**: `Show HN: <Name> – <what it does, plainly>`. No hype words, no emoji, no
  ALL CAPS, no "best", no exclamation marks. Under ~80 characters is safest.
  - ✅ `Show HN: Megaphone – score your GitHub repo's launch readiness`
  - ❌ `Show HN: Megaphone 🚀 The ULTIMATE AI-powered launch platform!!!`
- **URL**: the repo, or a live demo that needs no sign-up.
- **Text** (the submission's text field, or your first comment): 4–8 short paragraphs.
  1. One sentence on what it is.
  2. Why you built it — the specific itch.
  3. How it works (the interesting technical bit — this is what HN upvotes).
  4. What it doesn't do yet / known limitations.
  5. What feedback you'd like.

## Timing

No official guidance. A common community heuristic is a weekday morning, US time
(roughly 08:00–10:00 Pacific), when the "new" and "show" pages are watched by the most
people. More important than the hour: **be free for the next 3–4 hours** to answer
every comment.

## Template

```
Show HN: <Name> – <plain description>

Hi HN, I built <Name>, a <category> that <does X> for <audience>.

I made it because <specific situation>. The existing options were <A, B>, which
<concrete shortcoming>.

How it works: <the interesting part — algorithm, architecture, trick, constraint>.

It's <license>, written in <stack>. You can try it with:
    <one command or demo link>

What it doesn't do yet: <honest limitations>.

I'd love feedback on <specific question>.
```

## Day of

- Reply to every substantive comment, especially critical ones, calmly and specifically.
  Thank people for bug reports and fix fast — "fixed, thanks" mid-thread is great.
- Don't argue about downvotes or ask why the post isn't ranking.
- If it sinks without comments, don't delete and immediately repost. Wait, improve the
  project or the title, and try again much later — or email the mods (hn@ycombinator.com)
  politely; they sometimes put good posts in the second-chance pool.

## Pitfalls

- Linking to a page that needs sign-up → killed or ignored.
- Marketing tone in the text → top comment will call it out.
- Posting from a brand-new account with no comment history → often looks like spam.
- Asking for upvotes anywhere (X, Discord, WeChat groups) → can get the post penalised.
