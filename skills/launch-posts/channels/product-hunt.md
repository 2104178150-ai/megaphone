# Product Hunt

> Last checked: 2026-10 against Product Hunt's [launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch).
> Field limits aren't published there — confirm them in the submission form itself.
> Figures below marked *(commonly reported)* come from founder guides, not official docs.

## Fit

Products with a polished web presence that a broad tech/product audience can try
quickly: web apps, browser extensions, AI tools, developer tools with a hosted demo.
A bare library with no UI usually does poorly here — prefer Show HN and newsletters.

## Rules (from the launch guide)

- **You cannot ask people directly to upvote your product.** Share the link however you
  like, but ask people to visit and comment.
- Makers are encouraged to hunt (submit) their own products; Product Hunt says a
  third-party hunter gives no discernible advantage.
- Company accounts are prohibited — launch from a personal maker account.
- You can launch again when you have significant new product iterations.

## Format

- **Name**: product name only.
- **Tagline**: one short line *(commonly reported limit: 60 characters)*. Plain benefit,
  no emoji, no "AI-powered".
- **Description**: a few sentences on what it does and who it's for.
- **Gallery**: first image is the thumbnail most people see — make it show the product,
  not a logo. *(Commonly reported size: 1270×760.)* A short demo video helps.
- **Maker comment** (post it as soon as you launch): personal story — why you built it,
  what's different, what's free/open source, and a specific question for feedback.
- **Topics**: pick the most specific ones that fit.

## Timing

The launch guide recommends **12:01 am Pacific Time** if you can plan ahead, and says
the best day is the one you're most prepared for. Founder guides often prefer
Tuesday–Thursday for traffic, while weekends are quieter and less competitive. Avoid
launching the same day as your Show HN — you can't answer both well.

## Preparation (1–2 weeks out)

- Create or warm up your maker profile; follow and comment on other launches.
- Prepare all assets: thumbnail, 3–5 gallery images, 30–60s video, maker comment.
- Write a "we're live" message for your own channels that asks for **feedback**, not
  votes.

## Template — maker comment

```
Hey Product Hunt 👋 I'm <name>, the maker of <Product>.

I built it because <specific moment of pain>.

<Product> lets you <outcome> in <time/steps>. It's <free / open source under X>.

What's different from <alternative>: <one honest contrast>.

Would love your feedback on <specific question> — I'll be here all day.
```

## Pitfalls

- Launching an unfinished product → you only get one good launch day.
- Logo-only thumbnail → low click-through.
- Asking people to "upvote" → against the rules; ask them to "check it out and leave feedback".
