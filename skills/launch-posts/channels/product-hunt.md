# Product Hunt

> Last checked: 2026-10-18 against Product Hunt's [launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch),
> [Preparing for launch](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch) (field
> limits, timing), [Launching again](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/two-weeks-post-launch)
> and the [community guidelines](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/3615694-community-guidelines).

## Fit

Products with a polished web presence that a broad tech/product audience can try
quickly: web apps, browser extensions, AI tools, developer tools with a hosted demo.
A bare library with no UI usually does poorly here (heuristic) — prefer Show HN and
newsletters.

## Rules (from Product Hunt)

- **You cannot ask people directly to upvote your product.** Share the link however you
  like, but ask people to visit and comment. The community guidelines also ban mass
  messaging, bots and incentivised upvotes, and remove self-promotion in comments.
- Makers are encouraged to hunt (submit) their own products; Product Hunt says a
  third-party hunter gives "no discernible advantage".
- Company accounts are prohibited — launch from a personal account.
- You can launch again when you have significant new iterations. A resubmission within
  6 months of your last launch asks whether it's a major update and may need the team's
  approval.
- **URL**: a direct link to the product (a GitHub repo is accepted). No shortened links
  (bit.ly) or tracking links (UTMs).
- **Name**: only the product's name — no description, no emoji unless it's part of the name.

## Format

- **Tagline**: **max 60 characters**. "No gimmicks or over-the-top language"; plain
  benefit, no "AI-powered" (our advice, not a Product Hunt rule).
- **Description**: max 500 characters — what it does and who it's for.
- **Thumbnail** (required): square, 240×240 recommended, under 3 MB. A GIF animates only
  on hover, so its first frame is what most people see.
- **Gallery**: at least 2 images, 1270×760 recommended. Make the first one show the
  product, not a logo.
- **Video** (optional): YouTube links only. Product Hunt says about 53% of Product of the
  Day winners since 2021 had one.
- **First comment** (you can add it when you submit): key features, who it's for, your
  story, what feedback you want. Product Hunt says 70% of Product of the Day/Week/Month
  winners had a maker's first comment. Ask for feedback, not upvotes.
- **Makers**: add co-makers by their Product Hunt usernames; they need accounts well
  before launch day.
- **Topics**: pick the most specific ones that fit.

## Timing

- Product Hunt's homepage runs on a 24-hour cycle on Pacific time; its rule of thumb for
  makers without constraints is **12:01 am Pacific**. That's 07:01 UTC / 09:01 Berlin /
  15:01 Beijing / 16:01 Tokyo while the US is on daylight time, and 08:01 UTC / 09:01
  Berlin / 16:01 Beijing / 17:01 Tokyo on standard time. In the weeks when only the US is on
  daylight time (mid to late March, and late October to early November, e.g. 2026-10-25 →
  2026-11-01) it's 08:01 Berlin.
- Day: "The best day to launch is the day on which you're most prepared." Product Hunt
  says larger companies tend to launch on weekdays, while weekends can suit side
  projects and personal apps — and weekend launches get 15% more "Visit" clicks.
- You can schedule a launch up to 1 month ahead.
- Don't launch the same day as your Show HN — you can't answer both well (heuristic).

## Preparation (1–2 weeks out)

- Create or warm up your maker profile; follow and comment on other launches.
- Prepare the assets: thumbnail, 3–5 gallery images, a 30–60 s video if you have one,
  the first comment.
- Write a "we're live" message for your own channels that asks for **feedback**, not
  votes.

## Template — maker comment

```
Hey Product Hunt 👋 I'm <name>, the maker of <Product>.

I built it because <specific moment of pain — the author's own words>.

<Product> lets you <outcome> in <time/steps>. It's <free / open source under X>.

What's different from <alternative>: <one honest contrast>.

Would love your feedback on <specific question> — I'll be here all day.
```

## Pitfalls

- Launching an unfinished product → you spend your launch before the product is ready; a
  relaunch needs significant updates (and, within 6 months of your last launch, may need the
  team's approval).
- Logo-only thumbnail or first gallery image → low click-through.
- Asking people to "upvote" → against the rules; ask them to "check it out and leave feedback".
- A bit.ly or UTM-tagged URL → not accepted.
