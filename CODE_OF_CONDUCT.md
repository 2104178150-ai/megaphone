# Code of Conduct

Megaphone exists to help people share their work. We want the project itself to be a
place where anyone can do that without being made to feel small.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent, especially with first-time contributors and people
  writing in their second language.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Be specific about what would make it better.
- Welcome contributions in English or Chinese.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, including using this project's spaces to promote unrelated products.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs that break these rules, and may
block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem privately, contact the
maintainers through the email on their GitHub profile; reports are kept confidential.
