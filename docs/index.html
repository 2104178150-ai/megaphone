Megaphone's skills sharpen your pitch, rewrite the README's first screen and prepare the right post for each channel — a native draft, or a fact sheet where the platform wants your own words — for Show HN, Reddit, V2EX, HelloGitHub, 小红书 and more.

Get a dated launch plan, then line up star history and referrers against what you posted to see which channel actually worked.
